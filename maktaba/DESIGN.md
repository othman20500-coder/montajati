# Design: المجلد المذهّب

The visual world of «مكتبة آل عثمان», taken from the green gilt-bound volumes on the top shelf (التفسير البسيط، في ظلال القرآن).

- **Palette:** binding green `#173d2b` / `#1f5138` for the header bar, plates and primary buttons; gold `#c9a24a` / `#9c7c34` only for frames, rules and the focus of the search; light paper `#fbf8f1` ground, white `#fffdf8` panels; ink `#1b241f`, muted `#56605a`; warning `#9a4a12` on `#fbeadb`.
- **Type:** Amiri 700 for headings, book titles in the card and location numerals; IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400/500/600 for all text and controls.
- **Shell:** sticky green bar with brand mark (three gilt spines), search field and folder tabs whose selected tab joins the paper ground.
- **Signature move:** the location plate in the book card: a gilt-framed green plate showing the shelf code (أ٤), the position (٣٤) and «من اليسار», closed by a double gold rule.
- **Rules:** gold never carries text on light ground; state colours (review, warning) sit on top of the palette only where they mean something; the shelf drawing keeps the real spine colours.
